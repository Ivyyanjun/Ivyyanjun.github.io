I'm a researcher and instructor at China Central Academy of Fine Arts (CAFA). Before CAFA, I was a Postdoc Fellow at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, working under the guidance of Associate Professor [Xiaojun Ma (麻晓娟)](https://www.cse.ust.hk/~mxj/). Before joining HKUST, I worked with Professor Xin Wei Sha and Mina Johnson at Arizona State University; and Hiroshi Ishii's Tangible Media Group, MIT Media Lab. My research focuses on Human-Computer Interaction, responsive environments for children's social well-being, and Human-AI agent interaction for sociality. My current research interests lie in AI integration with design, exploring AI-mediated methods to promote children's social well-being.
